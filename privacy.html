Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Tyme Boxed ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, device, and companion app.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

  • Account Information: Email address and name when you create an account or pre-order.
  • Device Data: Information from your Tyme Boxed device when it syncs with our app.
  • Usage Data: How you use the app, including which apps you block and focus session data.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, and browser information when you visit our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Process orders and send order confirmations
  • Improve our products and user experience
  • Send important updates about your device and the app
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Focus and blocking data is stored locally on your device whenever possible. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyze site traffic. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
